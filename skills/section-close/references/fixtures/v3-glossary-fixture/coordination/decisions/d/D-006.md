## D-006 — Caps bind projections and the always-read tier, never the record

- **Context:** A byte cap on an append-only log is a category error. The cap breaches because the
  file is monotonic by design, and the pressure produces trimming, cap-raising, or archive-by-age.
- **Decision:** The record is uncapped. The generated index and the always-read tier carry the
  caps; the index breach action is to shard the generator, never to delete rows.
- **Rejected:** Raising the cap again (treats a structural mismatch as a parameter problem);
  summarizing old entries (measured loss, verbatim beats distilled retrieval by 16–22 points).
- **Consequences / evidence:** Supersedes D-002. Entry files are exempt from the mega-line rule.
