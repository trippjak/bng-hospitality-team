## D-007 — Archive-by-age is retired; supersession is the only path out of force
2026-08-21 • coordination/decisions/d/ • Cutting by age severs supersession chains, because the
superseding entry and the entry it replaces land in different cuts. A decision leaves force by
being superseded or reverted by a named later decision, and both ends resolve.
