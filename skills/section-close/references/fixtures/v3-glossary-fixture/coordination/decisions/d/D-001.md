## D-001 — Coordination docs are the durable tier; the conversation is volatile

- **Context:** Sessions are cleared. Anything a session knows that is not written to a tracked
  file is gone at the next clear, and a model-written summary is not a substitute for it.
- **Decision:** `coordination/` is the record. A close certifies safe-to-clear only when the
  claims a future session will act on trace to a file, not to prose in the transcript.
- **Rejected:** Compaction summaries as the carry-over vehicle — lossy, unverifiable, and they
  compound: a summary of a summary still reads fluently while drifting from the repo.
- **Consequences / evidence:** Every entry in this directory is one file, immutable after mint.
