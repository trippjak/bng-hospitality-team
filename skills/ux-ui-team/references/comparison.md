# Independent team comparison

Compare complete workflows first. The default Argon-led UX/UI Team and the separately managed Hugging Face experiments keep independent prompts, writable workspaces, artifacts, repair histories and conversations. Argon may lead one arm or serve as a declared frontier implementer in an experimental arm; sharing a model name never permits sharing task context or outputs. Do not modify, dispatch or message the other team/project without authorization. Updating this skill does not launch a trial.

Declare the experimental sequence explicitly. A specialist visual mock followed by frontier engineering is a different experiment from chaining two specialist generators. Render an authorized mock in isolation, treat it only as a visual reference, and have the experimental arm's frontier implementer preserve the real application's contracts. Neither successful inference nor a convincing screenshot permits merging raw generated code. Empty/truncated artifacts and failed stages remain recorded outcomes.

## Freeze the experiment

Before either arm starts, agree and pin:
- Same brief, code revision, brand assets, reference pack, acceptance tests, environment and viewports.
- Each arm's exact models/settings/tools and any screenshot-to-text transformation.
- Equal agreed elapsed/resource limits, one initial attempt and one repair; disclose unknown costs. Do not assume equal seeds/token accounting across providers.
- Same feedback rules and detail; never the other arm's implementation. No extra references or coaching for only one arm.
- Authoritative input/test hashes, independent output paths, final frozen manifests and evaluator-owned identity mapping.

No intermediate sketches, winning patterns or criticism crosses arms before both freeze. Shared inputs are read-only where possible. Worktrees prevent collisions but do not contain arbitrary generated code; follow acceptance's isolation rules.

## Judge

Independent evaluators receive neutral labels and the same rubric, withholding team/model identity where practical and recording unavoidable clues. The team's own orchestrator signoff does not replace independent comparison review. Critical behavior, accessibility and 4D are hard gates; high visual scores cannot offset failure.

For passing outputs rate usability, visual craft, brand fit and information clarity separately:
- **1:** fails the intended task or has major visible defects.
- **2:** substantial correction needed.
- **3:** usable, with noticeable weaknesses.
- **4:** strong, needing only minor refinements.
- **5:** polished/coherent, no material issue found in tested scope.

Give examples for ratings. Report time, known cost, repair count and human intervention separately. Retain failures and initial/final outputs; no undeclared best-of-N selection. Jake chooses the preferred output. One trial is task-specific evidence, not proof of a universally better model/team.

Separate delivery acceptance, reviewer preference and human preference. Unavailable models, isolation failures, exhausted budgets and missing evidence remain explicit outcomes, never silent exclusions/substitutions.
